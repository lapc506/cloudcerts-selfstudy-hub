# Code of Conduct — CloudCerts SelfStudy Hub

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)
v2.1. Our own wording, same spirit: make this a place people want to learn in.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback given
and received gracefully, owning mistakes, optimizing for the community over the ego —
and patience with learners (this is, after all, a study app).

Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or advances; trolling, insults, personal or
political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information
without consent; any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

All project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, Storybook reviews) and any situation where
someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the project maintainers with a description, links, and date. All
reports are handled confidentially. Consequences follow a progressive ladder:

1. **Correction** — private warning with explanation; public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — formal warning, no interaction with those involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction or communication with the community for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for patterns of violations, sustained harassment, or aggression
   toward individuals or groups.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith face the same ladder.
